Population · 10 drivers

Select a car to inspect its vision and neural network.

Neural network: 4 inputs (three sensors plus current speed) → 4 hidden neurons → steering, throttle and brake outputs, using weighted connections, biases and tanh activation. Training uses neuroevolution: distance and survival time create a score, then the champion's weights are mutated. A new champion is accepted only after beating the record by 8% (and at least 25 points). If a generation fails, the champion is kept and mutation strength increases. This is an educational simulation, not a real-world driving system.
